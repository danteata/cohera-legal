Cohera

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Cohera is a speaking-practice app. You are given a random word, you speak on it for a minute or two, and the app scores how clearly you spoke. This page explains exactly what that involves for your data.

The short version. Speech-to-text happens on your device. The text of what you said is sent to our servers to be scored and to two processors that turn it into feedback. Your recordings stay on your phone unless you save one. Nobody is shown your transcript alongside your name.

What Cohera collects

Your voice

When you record a practice session, audio is captured on your device and transcribed there, by your phone’s own speech recognition. The audio itself is not uploaded for transcription.

The recording stays on your device. It is only uploaded to our storage if you explicitly save or share a session.

Your transcript

The text of what you said is sent to our servers so the session can be scored, and is stored with your account so you can read it back later.

To score it and write feedback on it, that text is sent to two processors:

ProcessorWhat it receivesWhy
TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai)The transcript, the word you were given, the length of the sessionProduces the numeric scores
OpenAI or Google (Gemini)The sameWrites the sentences of written feedback

Your name, email address and account identifier are not sent to either. Neither processor receives your identity alongside the text.

If you turn on live coaching, short excerpts of the last 45 seconds are sent to TypeSafe every few seconds during the session, for the same purpose and on the same terms. Nothing from live coaching is stored.

Your account

If you sign in, our authentication provider (Clerk) processes your email address, display name and profile picture from the provider you chose.

You can use Cohera without an account. A signed-out session is scored on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Session data

For each session we store the word you were given, its length, your transcript, your scores, your filler-word count and speaking rate, and the written feedback. This is what your streak and progress are built from.

Usage analytics

We record in-app events — screens opened, sessions started and completed, scores — through Amplitude, tied to your account identifier. It does not include your transcript.

Purchases

If you subscribe, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive your subscription status, never your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your recordings or transcripts to train AI models.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not request camera, contacts or location access for practice sessions.

Where your data is held

Session data is stored with Convex. Processing takes place in the United States. If you are in the UK, EEA or another region with data-transfer rules, your data is transferred to and processed in the US.

How long we keep it

  • Sessions: kept while your account exists. Delete a session and it is removed.
  • Account data: deleted when you delete your account. You can do this yourself in the app — Profile → Delete account — and it takes effect immediately. See how to delete your account for exactly what goes and what is kept. Encrypted backups age out within 30 days.
  • Recordings: on your device, under your control, unless you chose to save one to your account.

Your rights

You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app, at Profile → Delete account, or by emailing us — see how to delete your account. You can also request access to, or correction of, your data at the address below. If you are in the UK or EEA, you also have the right to object to processing, to request portability, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Cohera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
