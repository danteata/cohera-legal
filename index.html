Delete your account

Cohera — impromptu speaking practice · Last updated 22 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Cohera account and everything stored with it. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete it from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect straight away.

  1. Open Cohera and sign in, if you are not already.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to Settings and tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be removed, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete my account.

You will be signed out as soon as it is done. There is no grace period and nothing is archived.

Or ask us to do it

If you cannot open the app, email dan.abakah@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and remove everything within 30 days, usually far sooner.

We ask you to write from the address on the account because it is the only way we can tell it is you. We will not ask you for a password, and we never have one — Cohera signs you in through Google or Apple.

What is deleted

Everything below is removed permanently, from our live systems, at the moment you confirm:

  • Every practice session: the transcript of what you said, your scores, the written feedback, the word you were given, the duration, your filler-word count and speaking rate.
  • Your progress: streak, level, XP and achievements.
  • Any saved recordings, including the underlying audio files in our storage.
  • Your profile: display name, email address and avatar.
  • Your sign-in record with our authentication provider, Clerk, so the account itself stops existing.
  • Everything the app stored on your device, including guest progress from before you signed in.

What is kept, and for how long

WhatHow longWhy
Encrypted database backups containing your rows Up to 30 days, then overwritten Backups roll on a fixed schedule and cannot be edited in place. Nothing is restored from them except to recover from a failure.
Anonymous usage counts (how many reps were taken, and when) Indefinitely Aggregated and not linked to you or to any transcript once your account is gone.
Google Play purchase records, if you subscribed As Google requires Held by Google, not by us. We never receive your payment details.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Google Play subscriptions are managed by Google and keep billing until you cancel them in the Play Store. Cancel first, then delete.

What we do not keep

Your transcripts are sent to our scoring and feedback processors while a session is being analysed, never alongside your name, email or account identifier. They are not retained by us after analysis beyond the session row itself, which this deletion removes.
